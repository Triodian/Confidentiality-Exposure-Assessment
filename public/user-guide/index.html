AI Confidentiality Exposure Assessment: user guide

For one live or recently closed transaction, the app records every channel by which matter information can reach an external AI model, and what the company could show about each if asked whether the information left.

1

What the app is for

The assessment is the technical half of a joint engagement led by the company’s counsel, whichever law firm that is; the firm is named in set-up. Counsel answers what the company can require of its people and advisers and what it must disclose if information leaves; the app records what can actually be shown. It is solution-agnostic: it presents no product, gives no legal advice and holds no matter content.

Each channel is one entry on the register. It goes through the five questions, is classified for reach and information class, is given an indicative rung on the non-egress evidence ladder (N0 to N6), and is tested rung by rung with the obtainability test (T1 to T5). Once counsel sets the required rung, each channel ends with one finding, and the residual gap (F5) is marked beside it wherever the channel accepts typed or pasted text.

Two standing rules run through every screen. Every legal determination is counsel’s: fields 23 and 24 are filled only from counsel’s written answers. And no matter content enters the assessment: the matter is referred to by a code name, and the app flags money amounts, large figures, percentages and named counterparties in any field.

2

Before you start

Everyone signs in through Cloudflare Access with their work email. An administrator adds each person in Admin and gives them one or more roles. What you can change depends on your roles, and on who did what: the server checks every save against the same rules the screens show. A disabled control carries its reason; the Why link explains it.

The worked example (menu → Practice) is a fictional deal that runs in the browser only. Use the Acting as picker to try each role. Nothing in it is saved.

Set your name, date format and time zone under Your profile. Your name is shown on the work you record.

3

Who does what

Method owner (Triodian)
Set-up and the profile, the legal-line and content check (R5), the annex text and signature. Can enter facts and classify.
Assessor (Triodian)
Facts (fields 1 to 14), classification and the obtainability test (15 to 22). Not on a channel they review.
Classification reviewer (Triodian)
R3: re-derives the reach from fields 4 and 8 alone, blind to the classifier’s work. Never on a channel they classified.
Validation (Triodian)
R4, only if they classified and reviewed nothing. Stands as R7 when no external checker is confirmed.
Delivery manager (Triodian)
Set-up, the profile, R2 (register completeness and content check), the exceptions log.
Counsel (the law firm)
Agrees the profile (R1), answers questions, R6, records the closure. Counsel sees every draft first.
GC or approving executive
Confirms the brief; agrees the profile (R1).
Second approver (company executive)
Only where the GC also acts as counsel: ticked in set-up as The GC also acts as counsel, with this person named. They give the second agreement to the profile (R1) in counsel’s place, and every output records that the GC held both roles. Without the setting, one person cannot give both agreements.
Matter team lead
Confirms who can reach each channel (question 1). Questions to advisers go through the matter team lead or counsel.
Tenant owner
Supplies the configuration export (D5); named on the 90-day steps that are configuration.
Independent checker
Logs, closes or discloses check points; confirms R7 when counsel has confirmed an external checker.

4

The eight phases

The Overview shows one card per phase with its gate: the conditions that must be met before the phase is closed. Work can carry on before a gate is met, but every output produced before its gate is met is marked draft. My actions lists what is waiting for you.

  1. Mobilise (before day 1). Set-up: the engagement, the people, the checker, the document request. The brief is confirmed by the GC.
  2. Matter profile (days 1 to 2). First choose the matter type: a deal, a litigation or dispute, an R&D or IP programme, a tender or bid, board papers, or export-controlled data. The type sets the wording of the profile, its stages, the adviser types and the information classes I1 to I6. If the board has set its confidentiality position in AI Board Direction, load that file on the Profile screen: each channel’s Counsel tab then shows a suggested required rung, marked “from the board’s position, counsel to confirm”. Counsel still sets field 24. The nine profile items under the code name: stage, team and insider counts, matter folders, advisers, sanctioned tools, device policy, privilege marking, counsel turnaround. The GC and counsel each record their agreement (R1). Every channel kind K1 to K7 must be considered on the register.
  3. Register the channels (days 2 to 5). One entry per channel, fields 1 to 14, each with a source or marked not known. A kind the company says does not exist is entered as absent, with the reason.
  4. Classify and test (days 5 to 8). Reach and class, the indicative rung, the obtainability test, confidence and the blind re-derivation. Validation (R4) and the five-minute demonstration are recorded.
  5. Questions to counsel (days 5 to 9). Every question answered, or left open with a named owner.
  6. Findings and plan (days 8 to 10). One finding per channel, the 90-day plan, R5 cleared, counsel review (R6).
  7. Check (day 10 or the following week). The checker’s points answered and closed; R7 confirmed.
  8. Close. The annex signed; counsel records the closure and directs what happens to the working papers.

5

Working on the register

Choose a kind and click Add channel, or Record as absent for a kind the company says does not exist. A K7 channel can also be added from each adviser on the Profile screen. Each channel opens in an editor with five tabs: Facts (1 to 14), Classify (15 to 17), Evidence (18 to 22), Counsel (23 to 24) and Finding (25).

Every field has a value and a source reference, or is marked Not known. A value without a source is flagged. Received documents and held interviews are offered as sources. A banned tool stays on the register with its status, and a K5 entry records where the use goes.

If a field looks like it holds matter content (a money amount, a figure in millions, a percentage, a named counterparty) the field shows a warning and the hit is listed under R5. Clear the field, or record an exception.

Where the documents do not show what a channel retains or reaches, tick Not determinable on documents and record the question that would resolve it and who could answer it. The channel is recorded as F4.

6

Classifying and testing a channel

The Classify screen works one channel at a time, in five steps.

  1. Reach and class. Answer the three reach tests; the app proposes the highest class the answers support. Record the reach (field 15). Choosing a different class needs a reason. Field 8 must be saved with a source first. Pick the highest information class the channel can reach (field 16). The matter team lead then confirms.
  2. Indicative rung. Set by rule: N4 for R1 and R2, N5 for R3 and R4, capped at N4 in an adviser’s estate and N1 on a personal device. Any further adjustment is recorded with its reason.
  3. Obtainability. Starting at the indicative rung, record pass or fail for each of T1 to T5, with the source. A rung that fails sends you to the next rung down. The first rung that passes every test is the obtainable rung (field 21). At B3 only T1 is asked.
  4. Confidence. Proposed from the T1 source: high where the export, log sample or attestation has been seen, medium where documentation describes it, low where inferred. You may lower it; raising it needs a reason.
  5. Blind re-derivation (R3). A second reviewer sets the reach from fields 4 and 8 alone. The classifier’s work is hidden from them until they record theirs. A difference is resolved by agreement, and both positions stay on the worksheet.

7

Questions to counsel

Use ask counsel beside any field, or the form on the Counsel screen: the channel, which of the five questions, the field the determination fills, the facts with sources, one question, and why it matters. Each question gets a reference (QC-01 onward) and counsel is emailed.

Counsel records the response in their own words, with a determination on reach and, for question 5, the required rung. Someone with a Triodian role may transcribe counsel’s written response word for word instead. Record in field 24 applies the newest answer to the channel and locks it; to change it, counsel answers a new question. On every B2 channel three points are flagged for counsel: sub-advisers and platforms, attestation, and whether the deed reaches what advisers do.

8

Review points and the legal-line check

The Review screen lists R1 to R7, open points first. R2 is run by the delivery manager and checks completeness, sources and matter content. R4 is recorded by a validation person who classified and reviewed nothing. R6 is counsel’s review of the draft. R7 is confirmed by the external checker, or by validation when none is confirmed, once every check point is closed or disclosed.

R5 scans every field and every output for three things: wording that states what the law requires, product names, and matter content. Each hit is listed once with its source and the outputs it appears in; the method owner clears it. No output can be exported as final until every hit is cleared.

The five-minute demonstration is recorded here: performed or not, on what text (described, never pasted), which controls the paraphrase passed. It is not an evidence item; the annex records it.

9 The engagement plan (EP) sets out the phases with their timing, the interviews and who attends, the documents needed from the company, and the sign-off points R1 to R7, with where each stands.

Outputs

Eight outputs are generated from the assessment: the channel register (CD1, also as CSV), the finding per channel (CD2), the 90-day plan (CD3), the residual gap (CD4), the technical annex (CD5), the question set (QS), the review pack for the check (RP) and the closing measures (CM). Each shows the privilege marking, the code name, draft or final, and the date. Download as Word, PDF or Markdown, or copy the Markdown.

The method owner writes the annex text for each of the twelve sections and signs the annex once the phase 5 gate is met. The 90-day plan lists every F2 step by owner and week; who closes F5, and with what, is a separate decision for the company and is not in the plan.

10

Closing, versions and another matter

A version is kept at each sign-off: profile agreed, counsel review, check complete, annex signed, closure. Compare versions on the Outputs screen redlines any version against a later one or against now. The register and annex end with the changes since the last version.

After the check, counsel records the closure and directs whether the working papers are archived (kept read-only under Earlier assessments) or destroyed. An administrator or the method owner can then start another matter assessment. The engagement details, the people, the sanctioned tools and device policy (marked for checking), and the document and interview lists carry over; everything about the matter itself starts afresh. If destruction was directed, the closed assessment and its saved revisions are removed.

11

Email notifications and your profile

A save that gives someone something to do sends them an email: a question to counsel, a reach to confirm or re-derive, an R3 difference, a channel recorded as not determinable, a check point or a response, counsel’s answer, the check complete. Counsel is reminded daily of questions open beyond the agreed turnaround. Emails carry channel ids and question references only. Turn them off under Your profile; the same items stay under My actions.

Your profile also holds your name and organisation, your email address (a change is confirmed by signing in with the new address), your date format and time zone, and your roles.
